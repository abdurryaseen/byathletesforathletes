Sound familiar?

Recruiting can be confusing. Most families figure it out too late.

For parents

“We do not know where to start”

Recruiting feels like a maze. Who do we email, and when? What should we be doing in each grade?

“We are paying for things that do not work”

Camps, showcases, and recruiting services add up fast, and it is hard to know what actually moves the needle.

“We are scared to start too late”

Other kids seem to be getting offers while ours has not heard from anyone, and the windows keep closing.

“Promises that never turn into offers”

Coaches say they love your athlete, then the offer never comes. Families are left guessing what is real interest and what is just talk.

“We do not understand the rules”

Dead periods, eligibility, NCAA requirements, and the transfer portal are confusing, and mistakes are costly.

For athletes

“My film is not getting me noticed”

A weak highlight reel gets skipped in the first few seconds, and coaches have hundreds to watch.

“I do not know what coaches want”

You are good, but are your numbers, measurables, and traits what programs are actually looking for?

“I send emails and get nothing back”

Most athletes do not know how to reach coaches or what to say when they do.

“My grades could hold me back”

Academics decide where you can play, and many athletes only find out when it is too late.

“I do not know how to handle setbacks”

Injuries, being overlooked, and the transfer portal can wreck confidence without a plan.

Meet your coaches

Athletes guiding athletes. Learn from the people behind By Athletes 4 Athletes.

Abdur-Rahmaan Yaseen

Co-Founder | Former Purdue & USF Wide Receiver

A four-star wide receiver out of Walled Lake Western in Michigan, Abdur-Rahmaan was the 2019 Associated Press Division 1-2 Player of the Year after catching 86 passes for 1,646 yards and 20 touchdowns as a senior, and he still holds his school records for career receptions, receiving yards, and receiving touchdowns. He earned 27 college offers and enrolled early at Purdue, where he was named Academic All-Big Ten, fought back from a season-ending injury, and later transferred to USF. Having lived the recruiting process, injuries, and the transfer portal firsthand, he started By Athletes 4 Athletes to help families understand what coaches look for and avoid the mistakes he learned from.

Broc Thompson

Frequently asked questions

Is this for middle school kids or younger?+

Yes. Recruiting starts earlier than most families expect, and coaches are already tracking talented athletes before high school. Starting in middle school or younger lets athletes build strong habits, learn what college coaches look for, and develop their mindset, athleticism, and grades long before recruiting gets competitive. Parents also get a head start on the process, so they can plan camps, film, and academics with confidence instead of scrambling in 9th grade.

Is this only for skill players, or for linemen too?+

Every position is welcome. Whether you play quarterback, receiver, running back, defensive back, offensive line, or defensive line, the recruiting process works the same way and every athlete needs the same foundation: knowing what college coaches look for, a strong highlight film, the right mindset, and a plan. Linemen and skill players alike get the education, coaching, and guidance to put themselves in front of the right programs.

Can I upgrade after starting with Recruiting Essentials?+

Yes. Recruiting Essentials is the perfect place to start. When you are ready for more, you can move up to Complete Athlete for athletic performance, whole-athlete development, and personal development planning, or go all the way to Elite Development for a personalized assessment, an individualized development plan, direct mentorship, and recruiting strategy. Each step builds on the one before it, so nothing you learn is wasted.

How does billing work?+

Every membership is billed monthly and renews automatically at the price shown until you cancel.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. You can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again after cancelling.

What is the difference between the tiers?+

Recruiting Essentials gives you the full How to Get Recruited video course, a grade-by-grade roadmap, a monthly live group coaching call, and the member community. Complete Athlete adds an onboarding call, nutrition, recovery, strength and speed training, development goal tracking, and a weekly live group coaching call. 5-Star Circle includes everything in both plans and adds 1-on-1 weekly film reviews during the season, a monthly 60-minute athlete diagnostic, an individualized recruiting roadmap, coach outreach strategy, access to private football coaches, X's and O's film room sessions, on-field and virtual position-specific training, mindset and confidence training, financial literacy, and life after ball.

When are the live mentorship sessions?+

Every membership includes a live monthly group coaching call with group Q&A and a member community.

Does this guarantee a scholarship or college offer?+

No. By Athletes 4 Athletes provides educational content only. Participation does not guarantee scholarships, college offers, roster spots, or recruiting outcomes.
